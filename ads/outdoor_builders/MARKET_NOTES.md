## Frame rate

Third-party spec guides agree that 9:16 H.264/AAC MP4 at 1080x1920 is the Reels spec and that 60 fps is accepted. They also say 30 fps is the commonly recommended setting and one says 60 fps input is re-encoded to 30. **No official Meta documentation was found**, so verify in Ads Help before relying on it.

Decision: the masters are now rendered at the source's native 60 fps as requested. Expect Meta paid placements to possibly transcode to 30 fps, so the 60 fps benefit may only show on organic posts and direct file delivery. A 30 fps master is cheap to re-render if upload checks complain.
