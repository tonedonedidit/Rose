## Why A is S5
- Island with stainless grill, fridge and stone veneer: 10–25 s.
- Ramada with TV and can-lights over it: 5–14 s.
- Fire pit with flame and seating: 42–50 s.
- Wide pullback that shows island, dining pad and fire pit in one frame: 59–64 s.
- No pool, no water, no night. That rules out S1 and a lights-on sequence, and it is why this ad is not sound-led.

## Structure: Three-beat list (#3), adapted
The brief's beats are scale, night lighting, zone connection. A has no night, so beat 2 is not lighting. The three beats are what a spec sheet can't show to a kitchen buyer, each proven by a matched shot:
1. **Placement.** Island under the ramada, sitting in its paver pad (src 10.0–14.0 s).
2. **Flow.** Pad, dining set, lawn and kitchen in one frame (src 63.4–66.0 s).
3. **The upsell.** Fire pit with flame on its own seating pad (src 45.2–48.8 s). This is pain #3 for the segment: upsells get skipped when they're only a line on a spec sheet.

Scale is proven by the hook shot and the wide frames, not named as a beat. Reason for keeping #3: beats map one-to-one to S5 pains #2 and #3, and each beat is a different zone of the yard.

## 3 concept variants
**V1 (chosen). Placement / Flow / Upsell.**
Hook over the grill, then three matched shots: island in its pad, pad-to-lawn flow, fire pit.
Ends on the CTA over the dimmed seating shot.

**V2. Scale / Placement / Heat.**
Opens on the 59–64 s pullback for scale, then island, then fire pit.
Rejected: the pullback is the weakest hook frame because the grill is small in it.

**V3. Spec sheet vs. yard.**
Cut between a text card styled as a spec line ("42-inch grill, stone veneer") and the matching shot.
Rejected: it invents spec-sheet copy and costs the first seconds on text, not on the island.

**Why V1:** it puts the stainless grill hero at frame 0, which is the thumb-stopper from the audit, and the beats double as the segment's pains. Every card is proven by a shot, so nothing in the copy goes beyond the footage.

## Competitor-paste test
- Hook 1: partial pass. The words "spec sheet" and "island" mark the kitchen builder, but a rival render shop could run the line with its own footage. What a rival cannot paste is frame 0 (this island) and the end card. Risk noted in VIDEO_SPEC.
- Hook 9 ("drawn by a guy who laid pavers") is the one fully unfakeable line. It is held back for the paver ad where it is the spine.
- Cards 2–5 each name a thing the footage shows. No card says anything a generic render shop could claim without the shot.

## CTA
**"Send us your next outdoor plan."** (31 characters, an instruction.) Also the instant-form headline. Wording identical across end card, form headline, button and primary text.
