## Delivery notes
- Pace: dry and level, like talking across a tailgate. No ad voice, no smile in the voice.
- Hit "spec sheet" in line 1 and "can't" in line 2.
- Breathe after "sits." in line 3. Let the island sit on screen for a beat before the next line.
- Line 5: lean on "skip".
- Last line: slow down, drop the pitch on "plan". Do not rush into the chime at 12.8.
- Record flat and close to the mic. Room tone under all the audio is fine; the music bed is quiet, around −24 dB.
