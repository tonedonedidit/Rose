# Stone & Rose: plan to get these two Reels to 50K and own the search

No one can guarantee 50K views. This plan stacks every lever that moves views, and it puts Stone & Rose in front of people who search for this service. The aim is the right 50K: Arizona contractors and homeowners, not random scrollers.

## Role I run as

Senior growth marketer for a one-service B2B2C studio. I position Stone & Rose as the supplier that sits between the sale and the build: renders for the buyer, HOA paperwork for the contractor. I pick the angle, write for retention and shares, and make the brand findable from every search path.

## The positioning idea: "the supplier behind every sold job"

Two audiences, one story.

- **Contractors (subs):** they sell the job, then wait on the HOA. We are the supplier who removes the wait. Pitch: one call, one file, your name on it.
- **Homeowners (buyers):** they hesitate because they can't picture the result. We are the first step before anyone digs.
- **Link between them:** each homeowner who sees H2 asks their contractor about renders. Each contractor who sees C2 sends us the job. Post both so the two audiences each hear about us from the other side.

### Market context (verify before using in any post)
Greater Phoenix has more homes for sale than a year ago and they sit longer. One broker-site report from late July 2026 shows about 15,100 active single-family listings, up roughly 59% year over year, and a 58-day median on market. The same report still calls it a seller's market, so do not say "buyer's market" in public copy. Source: [azbrian.com Greater Phoenix report](https://www.azbrian.com/greater-phoenix/home-market-report). Confirm with ARMLS data before quoting a number. Safe angle: when homes take longer to sell, a clear visual plan for the yard is a reason to choose one property or one contractor over another.

## Get found when people search the name or the service

1. **Instagram name field:** "Stone & Rose | Arizona 3D Landscape Design" (the name field is searchable, the handle is not enough).
2. **Bio:** "3D renders + HOA plans for Arizona backyards. Contractors: send us the job. Homeowners: start with renders. 👇" Link to one landing page, not the homepage.
3. **One handle everywhere.** Videos say @StoneandroseLLC, the website says @stoneandrose. Pick one, fix the other. [NEED: which is live.]
4. **Keywords in four places per Reel:** cover text, first line of the caption, on-screen text, and spoken or captioned audio. Alt text is already written in each caption file.
5. **Google Business Profile:** set it as a service-area business covering Arizona, categories for landscape designer and 3D rendering service, upload both Reels and 10 render photos, ask every client for a review. This is what shows when someone Googles the name.
6. **Website:** embed both Reels on a page each (HOA plans for contractors, 3D renders for homeowners), with the same wording as the caption and the disclaimer.
7. **Same Reels on Facebook, YouTube Shorts and TikTok** within 24 hours. More places to be found for the same search.
8. **Own the name search:** post the brand name in the caption of every Reel, and make sure Google shows the website, Instagram and Business Profile on page one for "Stone & Rose Landscaping Arizona."

## What drives views past 50K

| Re-cut | Make a 7-second version of each Reel with only the hook and the CTA. |

## 30-day cadence
- Week 1: post C2 and H2, set up the profile, Business Profile and cross-posts.
- Week 2: three short clips (one render reveal, one HOA plan walkthrough, one before/after).
- Week 3: Reel 3 for agents, plus a client review post.
- Week 4: re-post the best performer with a new hook, and boost it.

## What to track (weekly)
Views, watch time, sends, saves, profile visits, link clicks, DMs with HOA or RENDER, booked calls. If views are high and DMs are low, the CTA is weak. If views are low, change the hook.

## Compliance
Keep the disclaimer on every public post. Never promise an HOA approval, and never call the studio a contractor.
