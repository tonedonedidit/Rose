Questions

Contractor questions

What do you need from me to start a design?

The address, photos of the yard, and any measurements, plot plan or survey you have. Tell us the client's scope and budget range. If measurements are missing, we'll tell you exactly which ones we need.

Can the renders go to my client?

Yes. The renders are made for you to show your client and close the job. The spec sheet and build plans are for your crew and the HOA.

Do you compete with my company for the build?

No. On jobs you send us, the client stays yours. We design and document; you build.

How does pricing work?

Each job is quoted per project based on yard size and scope. Contractors who send work regularly can talk to us about ongoing rates.
