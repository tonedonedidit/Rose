About Stone & Rose

An Arizona landscape design studio that shows you the finished yard first

Stone & Rose is a design studio: we design your outdoor space and do not build it. We design high-end outdoor spaces around how you actually live: where you cook, where your family swims, where you sit in the evening. Each project starts with a conversation about your yard and your goals.

We combine detailed 3D visualization with knowledge of Arizona's climate, materials, and plant life, so the design you approve is one that holds up in the desert.

Questions

Common questions about working with Stone & Rose

What do I get with a Stone & Rose design?
Photorealistic 3D renders of your yard and a walk-through video are included with every design. A 360° video is an optional add-on.
Is the 360° video included?
No. The 360° video is optional. The 3D renders and the walk-through video are included.
How does the design process work?
It starts with a consultation about your yard and your goals. We then design the space in 3D, and you review the renders and the walk-through video before any construction begins.
Can I get 3D renders without a full design project?
Yes. 3D Render Only is one of the services you can choose on our contact form.
Do you build the project?
No. Stone & Rose is a design studio. We design your yard in 3D, and you take the approved design to the contractor of your choice.
Do you work with contractors and pool builders?
Yes. Contractors can bring us in to design the outdoor space for their client. We design and do not build, so the contractor keeps the construction work, and the client sees renders and a walk-through video before the build is quoted. See contractor partnerships.
Which parts of Arizona do you serve?
We design backyards, pools and outdoor kitchens in Scottsdale, Gilbert, Chandler, Mesa, Tempe, Queen Creek, Paradise Valley, Fountain Hills and Phoenix.
