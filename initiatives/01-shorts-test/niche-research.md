## Source 2: RPM benchmarks (NOT measured — vendor blogs, undisclosed samples)
ESTIMATE (based on blog benchmarks, long-form unless noted):
- Finance: ~$15–30 RPM — top tier, but low YouTube search interest above and "AI persona giving advice on sensitive topics" is flagged non-monetizable.
- True crime: ~$8–13 RPM; ads limited on sensitive content.
- Horror / scary stories: ~$4–8 RPM; volume play.
- Shorts pay far less than long-form per view (one source: finance Shorts ~$0.30–0.40 per 1,000).
Sources: outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-rpm-benchmarks, easyviral.ai (15 faceless ideas ranked by RPM 2026), blog.autonolab.com (RPM by niche).

## Source 3: Policy (secondary coverage; official page not read)
- July 2025: "repetitious" → "inauthentic content" (mass-produced/repetitive = no YPP). Enforced per channel.
- AI use itself is allowed; templated near-duplicates are not.
- July 2026 guidance adds generic/template content and AI personas advising on sensitive topics.
Sources: air.io monetization timeline, outlierkit.com faceless demonetized, logie.ai AI slop crackdown.

## Conclusion
- **Cut brain rot** — demand fell ~83% in a year, lowest RPM, highest "inauthentic" risk.
- **Channel A: Scary + AI stories** (original short fiction, e.g. AI/tech horror). Big stable demand + fastest-rising term; original fiction = no rights or defamation risk; cheapest to produce.
- **Channel B: True crime** (highest demand, higher RPM). Needs sourced facts and care.
- Finance parked: best RPM, weak YouTube search interest here, highest policy risk for AI narration.
