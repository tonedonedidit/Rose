Questions

Common questions

Can I see my backyard in 3D before anything is built?

Yes. Every design comes with photorealistic 3D renders of your actual yard, showing materials, plants, lighting and layout. You review them and ask for changes before any work starts.

Do you help with HOA approval?

Yes. We prepare the submittal package most Arizona HOAs ask for: a scaled site plan with measurements, a materials and color list, a plant list, and renders that show the finished look.

Can I get just the design and use my own contractor?

Yes. You can order the 3D design on its own. The package includes measured plans and a spec sheet your contractor can quote and build from.

What plants do you use for Arizona heat?

We plan around desert-adapted plants that handle full sun and summer heat with little water, such as agave, aloe, barrel cactus and saguaro, and place shade, turf and lighting where you will use them.

What areas do you serve?

Homes across the Phoenix metro area. Call (480) 973-5166 to check your city.
